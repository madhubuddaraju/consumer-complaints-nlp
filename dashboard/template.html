Method

DataArchived CFPB narratives, sampled per month; duplicates removed; scrubbing artifacts normalised.
SplitTemporal: train on older months, test on the latest 3. Harder than a random split, and closer to real use.
ModelsTF-IDF + logistic regression (runs in this page) vs fine-tuned DistilBERT, same test set.
AnalysisNear-duplicate detection, NMF topics with growth z-scores, auditable urgency rules.
